# 04 — Org & Talent

## Snapshot
The ops org enters H2 with seven functions reporting to Jeff: Eric (Manufacturing), Rasheen (Service Delivery — Field Logistics, Field Engineering, Field Operations, Customer Support), Lan (Purchasing), Anjana (Sourcing), Marques (Quality), Gaby (Finance), Ada (Operations Programs).

The load analysis in 03 says the half runs through three people: Anjana (both major cost levers), Eric (factory, fabrication, product fixes), and Marques (quality gate on four initiatives). The org plan exists to keep those three flowing.

## Structural change: Tech Services dissolved
Tech Services is deleted as a standalone function. Its work distributes to where it's closest to the constraint:
- **Engineering interface for redesign-to-cost** — no integrator seat at all. The work runs as a direct Engineering–supply-chain collaboration with Anjana accountable on the ops side. A broker between two teams that need to talk daily was a handoff the Algorithm says to remove.
- **Product fixes for the installed base** — Eric's manufacturing engineers, who already own design-for-build.
- **Field service system integrations** — stay inside Rasheen's org with vendor support.

One function deleted, zero of its load-bearing work orphaned, and the org's longest-standing three-way handoff (field → tech services → engineering) becomes two-way.

## H2 hires — each justified by the constraint it elevates
1. **Fabrication team, under Eric.** Required by Objective 3; sized by the part list 3A produces, not before. A senior fabrication lead is hired first (September) and builds out the team for October install — one experienced hire compresses the whole onboarding timeline. These roles are the explicit exception to the factory labor freeze.
2. **Cost engineer, under Anjana.** Keeps should-cost and quote evaluation moving while Anjana runs negotiations and the Engineering collaboration. With Anjana now carrying both major cost levers, this seat is the org's most urgent — July start, contract-to-hire if a search is slower.

**Not hired: a quality engineer.** Marques is the plan's hidden constraint candidate, but the response is structural: quality embeds at design release (1B) instead of qualifying after; Eric's manufacturing engineers author fabrication process qualification with Marques approving; and the deletions below clear his calendar. If Marques is still the bottleneck at the end of September, the hire question reopens with evidence.

## Freezes, deletions, consolidations
- **Tech Services deleted** (above) — the half's largest simplification.
- **Factory direct labor frozen** through the consolidation; fabrication roles exempt. Demand is soft; the consolidated layout should need fewer hands per unit, not more.
- **All open requisitions that predate this plan are cancelled** unless re-justified against one of the three objectives. Missing a hire and re-opening it later is cheaper than carrying it.
- **Ada's program office narrows to one program:** the cost program (1A, 1B, 3A, 3B as one thread). Standing program reviews not tied to the three objectives are deleted, not paused. Ada keeps the dependency map and the critical path.
- **Transactional sourcing moves to Lan's team** so Anjana's entire group is on cost-out.
- **Quality's non-essential calendar cleared:** internal audits and documentation projects not required by customers or regulators defer to 2027 — this funds Marques's critical-path load without a hire.

## Performance situation
One leadership seat adjacent to the cost program's critical path is not performing at the level H2 requires. The resolution path is decided by August and executed by early Q4, with interim coverage mapped before any action. Stated generically by design; this document circulates.

## Coverage risk
- **Anjana:** single accountable owner on both major cost levers. Relief is the July cost engineer; if that seat is empty past July, escalate staffing before milestones slip, not after.
- **Marques:** single point of approval on four initiatives, no backup approver. Mitigations above; named risk in 07.
- **Eric:** consolidation, fabrication, and product fixes land on one manufacturing engineering bench in the same half. If the bench can't cover all three, fabrication qualification has priority — installed-base fixes can borrow field engineering, fabrication can't.
- **Engineering outside ops:** the largest coverage risk isn't in the org at all. If their staffing commitment slips past August, Objective 1 loses its biggest lever — escalation pre-agreed in 1B.
- **Rasheen:** four functions plus two initiatives, partly self-relieving once legacy tools retire in October.
